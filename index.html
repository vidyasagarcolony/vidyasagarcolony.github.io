About us

Founded in 1950, Alipore Khelaghar Boys Club was born in a newly forming settlement that people then called Bangal Para. The families who built Vidyasagar Colony had come across the border after Partition: Hindu Bengalis from East Bengal, the Bangals. In the years that followed, Ghoti Bengalis, Bihari and Nepali families made this their home too. What started as a colony of displaced people became one para living in harmony since Independence. Many of our founding members were men who served at Alipore Jail, Presidency Jail and SSKM Hospital, people who understood duty, discipline and community service.

The club was their Khelaghar: a small room for adda, for football, for planning the next event. That spirit of service became our purpose. To keep the para together, we started organising what mattered most to us, not just Durga Puja, but Kali Puja, Lakshmi Puja, Saraswati Puja, Shivratri and Shitala Puja through the year. Along with the pujas came cultural programmes, sports tournaments and the simple work of looking after our neighbours.

More than six decades later, the name has changed from Bangal Para to Vidyasagar Colony, and the boys of 1950 are now elders, but the purpose remains the same. Alipore Khelaghar Boys Club is not just a puja committee. It is the thread that holds a multi-community para together, celebrating our roots while welcoming everyone as our own.
